Class Overview

Our Toddler program is built on the idea that children learn best when they play, explore, and create. At this stage, curiosity is natural, and every activity becomes a learning moment. Through songs, stories, sensory play, and movement, toddlers build the foundation for language, numbers, values, creativity, and social skills.

Description

The curriculum introduces early English, Math, and Urdu through fun, interactive methods — rhymes, stories, blocks, puzzles, and hands-on play. Each day includes Islamic storytelling to nurture values, outdoor play for physical growth, and activities that boost memory, problem-solving, and confidence. With repetition and structured play, toddlers develop habits of self-learning, observation, and discovery.

Toddler

Our goal is to make sure every child feels excited to come to school, knowing that each day is filled with discovery, laughter, and growth.
