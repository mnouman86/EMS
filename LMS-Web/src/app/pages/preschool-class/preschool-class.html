Class Overview

At The STEM Sprout School, our Preschool program is carefully designed for curious little learners aged 3–4 years. At this stage, children are eager to explore, discover, and express themselves. Our approach blends structured learning with play-based activities, ensuring kids develop essential early skills without feeling pressured or bored.

Through songs, stories, creative play, and hands-on practice, children build a strong foundation in language, numbers, problem-solving, and social interactions—all while enjoying every moment of their day.

Description

The Preschool curriculum focuses on self-learning through practice and encourages children to discover knowledge by doing, exploring, and experimenting. We integrate modern teaching methods such as interactive storytelling, digital learning tools, STEM-based activities, and memory-boosting games.

Subjects like English, Math, Urdu, Islamic Studies, and General Knowledge are introduced in simple, playful ways. Children also enjoy arts, music, outdoor play, and coding basics to develop creativity, confidence, and curiosity.

Every day includes revision time, healthy physical activities, and mind-sharpening exercises, ensuring children not only learn but also grow in confidence, imagination, and character.
